MATH 2201 — Midterm Formula Sheet

Units 1–3  ·  vectors, lines & planes, vector-valued functions, multiple integrals

Vector Basics

$$ \mathbf{a} = \langle a_1,a_2,a_3\rangle = a_1\mathbf{i}+a_2\mathbf{j}+a_3\mathbf{k} $$
$$ |\mathbf{a}| = \sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2} $$
$$ \hat{\mathbf{a}} = \frac{\mathbf{a}}{|\mathbf{a}|} \quad(\text{unit vector}) $$
$$ \overrightarrow{AB} = B - A $$
$$ |AB| = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2+(z_2-z_1)^2} $$
Head minus tail: \(\overrightarrow{AB} = B-A\), never \(A-B\).

Dot Product → scalar

$$ \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3 $$
$$ \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} = |\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\cos\theta $$
$$ \cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|} $$
Projections of a onto b
$$ \text{comp}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{b}|} \quad(\text{scalar}) $$
$$ \text{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{b}|^2}\,\mathbf{b} \quad(\text{vector}) $$
\(\mathbf{a}\perp\mathbf{b} \iff \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=0\)

Cross Product → vector

$$ \mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \end{vmatrix} $$
$$ = (a_2b_3-a_3b_2)\mathbf{i} - (a_1b_3-a_3b_1)\mathbf{j} + (a_1b_2-a_2b_1)\mathbf{k} $$
$$ |\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}| = |\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta $$
Area, parallelogram\(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|\)
Area, triangle \(ABC\)\(\tfrac12|\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}|\)
Normal to a surface\(\mathbf{n}=\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}\)
\(\mathbf{a}\parallel\mathbf{b}\)\(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}=\mathbf{0}\)
The \(\mathbf{j}\) term is minus.

Scalar Triple Product

$$ \mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c}) = \begin{vmatrix} a_1 & a_2 & a_3 \\ b_1 & b_2 & b_3 \\ c_1 & c_2 & c_3 \end{vmatrix} $$
$$ V_{\text{parallelepiped}} = \big|\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})\big| $$
Coplanar \(\iff \mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c}) = 0\) (zero volume).
Volume takes the modulus — never negative.

Lines in 3-Space

Vector form
$$ \mathbf{r} = \mathbf{a} + \lambda\mathbf{b} $$
Parametric
$$ x=a_1+\lambda b_1,\;\; y=a_2+\lambda b_2,\;\; z=a_3+\lambda b_3 $$
Symmetric
$$ \frac{x-a_1}{b_1}=\frac{y-a_2}{b_2}=\frac{z-a_3}{b_3} $$
Through \(A\) and \(B\): use \(\mathbf{a}=A\), \(\mathbf{b}=B-A\).

Planes

Parametric
$$ \mathbf{r} = \mathbf{a} + s\mathbf{u} + t\mathbf{v} $$
Normal form
$$ (\mathbf{r}-\mathbf{a})\cdot\mathbf{n}=0 \;\;\Leftrightarrow\;\; \mathbf{r}\cdot\mathbf{n}=\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{n} $$
Cartesian
$$ n_1x+n_2y+n_3z = d,\qquad d=\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{n} $$
Through 3 points A, B, C
$$ \mathbf{n} = \overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC} $$
Read \(\mathbf{n}\) straight off the coefficients of \(x,y,z\).

Angles — Cos or Sin?

Plane ↔ plane · line ↔ line
$$ \cos\theta = \frac{|\mathbf{n_1}\cdot\mathbf{n_2}|}{|\mathbf{n_1}||\mathbf{n_2}|} $$
Line ↔ plane
$$ \sin\theta = \frac{|\mathbf{d}\cdot\mathbf{n}|}{|\mathbf{d}||\mathbf{n}|} $$
The normal sticks out of the plane, so line–plane needs the complement — sine does that flip.
Keep \(|\cdot|\) on top for the acute angle.

Perpendicular / Parallel

⟂∥
Plane–plane\(\mathbf{n_1}\cdot\mathbf{n_2}=0\)\(\mathbf{n_1}=\lambda\mathbf{n_2}\)
Line–plane\(\mathbf{d}=\lambda\mathbf{n}\)\(\mathbf{d}\cdot\mathbf{n}=0\)
Line–line\(\mathbf{d_1}\cdot\mathbf{d_2}=0\)\(\mathbf{d_1}=\lambda\mathbf{d_2}\)
Line–plane row is swapped vs the others.

Distance & Intersection

Point to plane · Ax+By+Cz = D
$$ \text{dist} = \frac{|Ax_1+By_1+Cz_1-D|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2+C^2}} $$
Written \(Ax+By+Cz+d=0\) instead? Then add \(d\). Pick one form and stay in it.
Line meets plane
  • Write line parametrically
  • Substitute \(x,y,z\) into the plane
  • Solve for \(t\), put \(t\) back in the line
Horizontal plane \(z=c\): only the \(z\)-component matters.

Vector-Valued Functions

$$ \mathbf{r}(t) = x(t)\mathbf{i}+y(t)\mathbf{j}+z(t)\mathbf{k} $$
$$ \mathbf{r}'(t) = x'(t)\mathbf{i}+y'(t)\mathbf{j}+z'(t)\mathbf{k} $$
Differentiate each component separately. That's the whole rule.
Velocity\(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{r}'(t)\)
Speed\(|\mathbf{r}'(t)|\)  (scalar)
Acceleration\(\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{r}''(t)\)
Unit tangent\(\mathbf{T}=\mathbf{r}'/|\mathbf{r}'|\)

Rules of Differentiation

1\(\frac{d}{dt}\mathbf{c} = \mathbf{0}\)
2\(\frac{d}{dt}(k\mathbf{r}) = k\mathbf{r}'\)
3\(\frac{d}{dt}(\mathbf{r_1}+\mathbf{r_2}) = \mathbf{r_1}'+\mathbf{r_2}'\)
4\(\frac{d}{dt}(\mathbf{r_1}-\mathbf{r_2}) = \mathbf{r_1}'-\mathbf{r_2}'\)
5\(\frac{d}{dt}(a\mathbf{r_1}+b\mathbf{r_2}) = a\mathbf{r_1}'+b\mathbf{r_2}'\)
6\(\frac{d}{dt}\big(f(t)\mathbf{r}\big) = f'\mathbf{r}+f\mathbf{r}'\)
\(\mathbf{c}\) constant vector; \(a,b,k\) constants; \(f\) any function of \(t\).

Arc Length

$$ ds = \sqrt{[x'(t)]^2+[y'(t)]^2+[z'(t)]^2}\,dt $$
$$ L = \int_a^b \sqrt{[x']^2+[y']^2+[z']^2}\;dt = \int_a^b |\mathbf{r}'(t)|\,dt $$
The integrand is the speed: differentiate → magnitude → integrate.
Standard results
Circle \(\langle a\cos t, a\sin t\rangle\)\(|\mathbf{r}'| = a\)
Helix \(\langle a\cos t, a\sin t, ct\rangle\)\(|\mathbf{r}'| = \sqrt{a^2+c^2}\)
Simplify with \(\sin^2+\cos^2=1\) before integrating — the root nearly always collapses to a constant.
If it doesn't collapse
$$ \int\!\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\,dx = \frac{x}{2}\sqrt{x^2+a^2}+\frac{a^2}{2}\ln\!\left(x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2}\right)+c $$

Double Integrals

$$ \iint_R f(x,y)\,dA = \text{volume under } z=f(x,y) $$
Over a rectangle [a,b]×[c,d]
$$ \int_a^b\!\!\int_c^d f\,dy\,dx = \int_c^d\!\!\int_a^b f\,dx\,dy $$
Fubini: with constant limits the order doesn't matter — pick the easier one.
General region
$$ \int_a^b\!\!\int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)} f(x,y)\,dy\,dx $$
$$ \text{Area of } R = \iint_R dA $$
Outer limits are always constants. A variable left in your answer = wrong order.

Triple Integrals

$$ \iiint_V f(x,y,z)\,dV, \qquad dV = dx\,dy\,dz $$
$$ V = \iiint_V 1\,dV $$
Integrand \(=1\) gives the plain volume of the solid.
Method
  • Innermost integral first
  • Treat the other variables as constants
  • Substitute limits, move outward
All-constant limits on a box: the answer is just the product of the side lengths — use it to check your setup.

Final Checklist

  • Minus sign on the \(\mathbf{j}\) term of every cross product
  • Modulus on volume, and inside every angle formula
  • Sine line–plane, cosine plane–plane
  • \(\overrightarrow{AB}=B-A\)
  • \(\tfrac12\) for triangles only
  • Simplify \(|\mathbf{r}'(t)|\) before integrating
  • Inner limits belong to the inner differential
  • Substitute given points back into your plane
  • Lengths, areas, volumes are never negative
  • Units: m³, seconds, square units
