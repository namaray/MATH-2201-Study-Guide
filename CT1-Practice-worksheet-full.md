# CT1 Practice Worksheet — Vectors

**Q1** Two aircraft depart simultaneously from the same airport. Their flight directions are represented by the vectors

a = 3i + 2j + 6k,
b = 2i - 2j + 5k.

Find the angle between the two flight paths.

---

**Q2** A robotic arm has two connecting rods represented by

a = 4i + 2j - 6k,
b = 3i - 6j - 2k.

Show whether the rods are perpendicular.

---

**Q3** Two supporting beams of a rectangular solar panel are represented by

a = 2i + 3j + k,
b = 4i - j + 2k.

Find the area of the parallelogram formed by the two beams.

---

**Q4** Three corner points of a triangular park are A(1,2,1), B(4,3,5), C(2,7,4).

Find the area enclosed by the three points.

---

**Q5** A drone starts from the point P(2,-1,4) and moves in the direction d = 3i - 2j + k.

Find
(a) the vector equation,
(b) the parametric equations, and
(c) the symmetric equation of the line representing the drone's flight path.

---

**Q6** The edges of a roof are represented by

a = 2i + j - k,
b = i + 4j + 2k.

Find a vector normal to the roof surface. Hence determine a unit normal vector.

---

**Q7** The velocity of the wind is W = 6i + 4j + 2k (m/s) while the direction of a runway is R = 3i + 4j.

Find
(a) the vector projection of the wind on the runway,
(b) the scalar component of the wind along the runway.
